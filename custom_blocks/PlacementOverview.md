A <Glossary>placement</Glossary> represents a specific location in your user journey inside your app (e.g. Onboarding, Settings, Home page, Article). A <Glossary>placement</Glossary> is linked to a Screen and a single Screen can be used for different Placements. You can create as many Placements as you want, and this is the only thing that ties the app developer to the marketer.

Placements are configured in the section [Placements of the Purchasely Console](https://console.purchasely.io/placements). From there, you can get the placement ID by clicking on the Copy button.
