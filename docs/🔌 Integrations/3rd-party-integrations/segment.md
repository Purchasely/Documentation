# Integration

To integrate Purchasely with your Segment account please follow the instructions below:

1. In your Segment dashboard, click on Add a new source
2. <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/9c836a9-image.png" />

   In the upcoming screen, Select the `“HTTP API”`

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/9303311-image.png" />
3. Provide the name of your source
4. <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/0a4992e-image.png" />

   Copy the Segment Write Key as shown in the screenshot below

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/ccf59b6-image.png" />
5. In the Purchasely console, go to **"External integrations"** section, and open the edition form for Segment

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/5bb010a-Capture_decran_2024-07-23_a_15.52.50.png" />

6. Enable the integration and set your Android/iOS `API key` 

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/68bc203-Capture_decran_2024-07-23_a_15.53.06.png" />

7. Save
