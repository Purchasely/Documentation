Purchasely is composed of 3 modules that can be used independently :

* **Transaction infrastructure**: transaction management, subscriber status, content and services unlocking to handle the subscription on all stores and devices and make sure very subscriber has the right credentials
* **Subscription Data Hub**: Monetization dashboards, subscriber lifecycle events, 3rd party integrations to enrich your existing stack with precise and real time data and to understand and pilot your growth
* **No-code Experimentation Lab**: Our unique technology to generate native screens and funnels, A/B tests,  and segment to customers audiences. 

<br />

You might have an existing subscription management system and changing it might seem complex to your organisation.\
With our modular approach you can leverage the entire power of our No-Code Experimentation Lab on your existing infrastructure in minutes **only involving mobile developers**. This is what`paywallObserver`mode is made for.

This mode allows you to take advantage of the Purchasely SDK of the following features without migrating your existing Subscription Infrastructure:

* No-code native paywall & in-app screen builder
* A/B test module
* Audience targeting capabilities
* Campaign automations
* 3rd party integrations

<br />

Purchasely will handle everything and handover to you when a transaction needs to be processed.\
Every necessary information including store product id and promotional offer informations will be provided.
