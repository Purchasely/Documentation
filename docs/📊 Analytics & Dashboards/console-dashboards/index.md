The Purchasely Dashboard allows you to follow the most important metrics of your In-App Purchase and In-App Subscription business. It is split in different views:

* [The Live Dashboard](dashboard-live) provides you with an instant snapshot of your subscriber base
* [The Conversion Dashboard](dashboard-conversion) allows you to analyze the Conversion, either globally for your app or for a specific Paywall / Country etc...
* [The Subscriptions Dashboard](dashboard-subscriptions) gives you details on your business pulse and revenues
* [The One-Time Purchases Dashboard](dashboard-otp) gives you details on your in-app purchases sales and revenues
* [The MRR Dashboard](dashboard-mrr)  allows you to follow the Monthly Recurring Revenue
* [The Cohorts Dashboard](dashboard-cohorts) helps you measure Subscription retention
* [The Trials Dashboard](dashboard-trials) gives you details on Introductory Offers conversion
* [The Promotional Offers Dashboard](dashboard-promotional-offers) gives you details on Promotional Offers conversion
* [The Events Dashboard](dashboard-events) is an experimental tool to visualize the [Server Events](server-events) generated by the Platform and identify growth opportunities
