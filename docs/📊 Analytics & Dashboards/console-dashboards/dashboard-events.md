This tool allows you to identify business opportunities by analyzing the number of occurrences of specific Events in the subscription lifecycle.

For instance, by focusing on Billing issues, you can assess the daily/weekly/monthly volumes of Grace period, and the proportion of times where they are recovered and determine whether it would be worth it or not to create an automation to remind the users to update their billing details to avoid losing their premium benefits while they are in Grace period.
