## Why configure an email domain?

Redemption and lifecycle emails are sent from your brand (e.g. `welcome@sportelo.com`) instead of a Purchasely address — better trust and deliverability. Until configured, emails are sent from a Purchasely domain.

## What DNS records do I need?

After saving your domain, we generate SPF and DKIM records to add at your DNS provider so we can send on your behalf. [What are SPF and DKIM?](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/email-security/dmarc-dkim-spf/)

## Can I change it later?

Yes, the sending domain and mailbox can be updated at any time.
