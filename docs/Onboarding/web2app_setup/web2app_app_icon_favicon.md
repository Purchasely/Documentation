## Where are they used?

On your web funnels — redemption screen, download prompts, browser tab and bookmarks — so the web journey matches your app.

## Can I reuse my app icon?

Yes, we pull it from your app settings by default. You can upload a dedicated image instead.

## What formats and sizes?

PNG or SVG, square. App icon ≥ 512×512 px, favicon ≥ 48×48 px — we generate all required sizes.
