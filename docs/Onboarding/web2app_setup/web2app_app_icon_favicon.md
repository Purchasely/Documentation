# Where are the app icon and favicon used?

The icon appears on the success screen, the download prompts and the receipt email; the favicon in the browser tab and bookmarks of your funnels. They make the web journey look like your app. See [Setup 3 · Verify your mobile app](web2app-setup-mobile-app).

# Do I have to upload an icon?

No. Your funnels use the app icon from App settings → General by default. Upload one here only if you want a different visual on the web; it updates your app settings as well.

# What formats and sizes?

Square PNG or SVG. App icon at least 512×512 px, favicon at least 48×48 px. Purchasely generates every size it needs.

# The favicon does not change in my browser

Browsers cache favicons aggressively. Open the funnel in a private window or another browser.
