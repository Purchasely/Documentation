# What is a webhook?

A webhook is an automated interface carrying messages sent from a platform (the sender) to a unique URL (the recipient endpoint) when something happens. They're much like SMS notifications and contrary to an API, there is no need to poll it regularly to receive a message.

In the case of the Purchasely webhook, the messages sent are events related to the transactions that are processed by the app store (or stripe) and the subscriptions lifecycle. The recipient is of these messages are the developer backend.

# What's the Purchasely webhook used for?

The Purchasely webhook can be used for 2 different purposes:

1. **Managing the entitlements of the users**: 2 messages (**`ACTIVATE`** / **`DEACTIVATE`**) are sent when a new subscription starts or when an existing subscription terminates.
2. **Fetching the subscription lifecycle**: subscription events can be sent to the webhook interface with associated revenue information

For the developer, the benefits of integrating the webhook are the following:

1. having a unified interface to manage consistently the entitlements on all platforms (iOS, Android, web) by simply leveraging 2 simple messages `ACTIVATE` / `DEACTIVATE`) which avoids to integrate the complex server-to-server notifications with each store.
2. leveraging the [subscription events](https://purchasely.readme.io/docs/analytics_subscription_events) generated by the Purchasely platform all along the subscription lifecycle, to create automated retention or win-back campaign on specific moments of the subscribers lifecycle, such as:
   - the deactivation of the auto-renewing of their subscription (event `RENEWAL_DISABLED`)
   - the entrance in the grace period or billing retry following a billing issue (events `GRACE_PERIOD_STARTED`)
   - the renewing of a subscription (event `SUBSCRIPTION_RENEWED`)

More information about the subscription events is available here: [Subscription events](https://purchasely.readme.io/docs/analytics_subscription_events)

# Receiving and acknowledging webhook messages

Messages sent on the webhook by the Purchasely platform need to be acknowledged by the developer backend to confirm to the Purchasely Platform that they have been processed.

This acknowledgement is done by responding to the message with a `HTTP 200` response code.

After the Purchasely Platform has received the response, it notifies the Purchasely SDK to confirm to the user that they are now a premium member and can have access to the premium contents / features. 

The Purchasely Platform implements a **retry mechanism** when a message is not acknowledged to prevent the risk to lose any message.

When a message is not acknowledge, the Purchasely SDK gets notified that something went wrong while granting the entitlements and the user is invited to retry in a few minutes.
