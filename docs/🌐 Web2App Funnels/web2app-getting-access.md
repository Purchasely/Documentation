Web2App Funnels are enabled **per Purchasely app**. Once enabled, a **Web 2 App** entry appears in the sidebar of the Console for that app, with the Setup and Flows tabs described in this section.

## Request access

Reach out to your **Customer Success manager**, or to your **Account Executive** if you are evaluating Purchasely, and tell them which app you want to enable. They will walk you through the commercial terms and activate the feature on the app.

Before the call, it helps to have in mind:

* the **Stripe account** you will use, or whether you need to create one;
* the **plans** you want to sell on the web;
* the **platforms** of your app and their current **Purchasely SDK version**, since 6.1 or later is required to activate web subscriptions;
* the **ad platforms** you run campaigns on.

## What happens next

1. Your Customer Success manager enables Web2App Funnels on your app.
2. You complete the guided setup in **Web2App → Setup**: [Stripe](web2app-setup-stripe), [domains and wallets](web2app-setup-domains-and-wallets), [mobile app](web2app-setup-mobile-app), [ad platforms](web2app-setup-ad-integrations).
3. You [build your first Web Flow](web2app-build-a-web-flow), [test it in sandbox](web2app-test-and-go-live) and point your first campaign to its live URL.

Your Customer Success manager stays available throughout the setup and for the review of your first funnels.
