Web2App Funnels must be **enabled on your Purchasely account** before you can use them. Once your account is enabled, a **Web 2 App** entry appears in the sidebar of the Console for each of your apps, with the Setup and Flows tabs described in this section. The setup itself (Stripe, domains, mobile app, integrations) is then done app by app.

## Request access

Reach out to your **Customer Success manager**, or to your **Account Executive** if you are evaluating Purchasely, and tell them which apps you plan to run web funnels for. They will walk you through the commercial terms and enable Web2App Funnels on your account.

Before the call, it helps to have in mind:

* the **Stripe account** you will use for each app, or whether you need to create one;
* the **plans** you want to sell on the web;
* the **platforms** of your apps and their current **Purchasely SDK version**, since 6.1 or later is required to activate web subscriptions;
* the **ad platforms** you run campaigns on.

## What happens next

1. Your Customer Success manager enables Web2App Funnels on your account.
2. For each app, you complete the guided setup in **Web2App → Setup**: [Stripe](web2app-setup-stripe), [domains and wallets](web2app-setup-domains-and-wallets), [mobile app](web2app-setup-mobile-app), [ad platforms](web2app-setup-ad-integrations).
3. You [build your first Web Flow](web2app-build-a-web-flow), [test it in sandbox](web2app-test-and-go-live) and point your first campaign to its live URL.

Your Customer Success manager stays available throughout the setup and for the review of your first funnels.
