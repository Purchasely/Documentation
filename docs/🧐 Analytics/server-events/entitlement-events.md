Entitlement Events are generated by the Purchasely Platform when premium accesses are created/modified/removed (because of a purchase, renewal, refund, payment issue, ...).

There are 2 different Entitlements Events:

| Event | Used to | Triggered when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ACTIVATE` | Activate the user's entitlements | 1. a new subscription starts<br>2. a subscription is renewed<br>3. an In-App Purchase (consumable / non consumable) is purchased<br>4. a subscription or a non consumable is "stolen" from another user, following a restore or sign-in/sign-up<br>5. a subscription starts because of a plan change (up-selling, down-selling or cross-selling)<br>6. a subscription enters in grace period<br>7. a subscription is resumed after being paused (Google Play Store only)<br>8. a subscription is resumed after a billing retry or a grace period<br>9. a refund has been asked by the user via Apple Care but the developer refuses it from the App Store Connect Console (tricky one we know 😅...) |
| `DEACTIVATE` | Deactivate the user's entitlements | 1. a subscription is effectively terminated<br>2. a subscription enters in billing retry<br>3. a subscription or an In-App Purchase has been refunded<br>4. a subscription or a non consumable has been transferred to another user, following a restore or sign-in/sign-up<br>5. a subscription ends because of a plan change (up-selling, down-selling or cross-selling)<br>6. a subscription is being paused (Google Play Store only) |

<br />

They are both composed of a JSON payload which includes all the relevant data to manage entitlements ([more details on their attributes](server-events-attributes)).

For more information on how to manage entitlements with your backend, refer to [Backend entitlements](backend-entitlements).
