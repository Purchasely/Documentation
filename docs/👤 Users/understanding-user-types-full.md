When dealing with In App Purchases & Purchasely, we encounter three types of users:

* **Store** user: an account used for making purchases on the stores (App Store, Play Store, ...).
* **Application** user: a user of your application.
* **Purchasely** user: a user to whom purchases will be linked in Purchasely's servers.

<br />

So, for example, as an iPhone owner, I might:

* have created an account on the **App Store** with one email [email\_1@my\_provider.com](mailto:email_1@my_provider.com).
* have created an account on **your application** with another email [email\_2@my\_provider.com](mailto:email_2@my_provider.com) (which corresponds, for example, to the user with the ID 12345 in your database).
* have a subscription on **Purchasely** associated with the ID 12345 (as you would have provided this ID to the Purchasely SDK).

<br />

Thus, we will have three distinct users:

* Store: (email=[email\_1@my\_provider.com](mailto:email_1@my_provider.com))
* Application: (id=12345, email=[email\_2@my\_provider.com](mailto:email_2@my_provider.com))
* Purchasely: (id=12345)

<br />

Let's dig into these different user types.

<br />

# Store user

Corresponds to the account used by a person to make purchases on a store (App Store for Apple, Play Store for Google).

> 📘 Good to know
>
> * This account is managed by the store (your application and Purchasely have no control over it).
> * The email associated to this account will receive the invoices from the stores.
> * It is impossible to make a purchase on these stores without creating an account.

<br />

# Application user

Corresponds to a user of your application.

This user can be either logged in or not. Typically, news applications might let you use them without needing to create an account; these users are called “anonymous users”. Otherwise, they are called “connected users”.

<br />

# Purchasely users

Purchasely’s servers have several ways to become aware of a new purchase:

* via the Purchasely SDK
* via a [manual import](subscribers-base-import)
* via store notifications

In the first two cases, since the information is provided by the SDK or your own servers, Purchasely’s servers know the user’s identity. These users are referred to as **known users** (whether they are connected or not).

However, when the information comes from a store notification, Purchasely’s servers have no way of knowing the subscriber’s identity. These are referred to as **unknown users**.

<br />

## Known Users

The purchase is made ([full mode](full-mode)) / declared ([observer mode](paywallobserver-mode)) to the Purchasely SDK. It can also [be imported manually](subscribers-base-import).

As previously discussed for “Application users”, these users can be **anonymous** or **connected**.

<br />

### Connected Users

Subscribers who log in to your app and are [declared as such](user-identification#authenticate-users) to Purchasely.

Every webhook or data displayed in the Purchasely console for this type of user will be associated with the user’s identifier.

<br />

### Anonymous Users

Subscribers who [do not log in](user-identification#anonymous-users) to your app.

An anonymous ID is generated by the Purchasely SDK and will be associated with the purchases.

<br />

## Unknown Users

The purchase is known to Purchasely’s servers following the reception of a <Glossary>S2S</Glossary> notification. Purchasely has no way to know the identity of the buyer.

For this type of user:

* No Purchasely webhook will be sent to you.
* No data will be transmitted to the various integrations you may have configured.
* The [S2S transfer](s2s-notifications-forwarding) will remain functional.

<br />

### When are Unknown Users created?

* The purchase is made on a version of your app not using the Purchasely SDK.
* The subscription was obtained through family sharing.
* An error occurred during the purchase (app closed during the purchase, network issue).
* You are in [observer mode](paywallobserver-mode) and do not call [Purchasely.synchronize()](process-transactions-with-paywall-action-interceptor#processing-transactions-with-your-in-house-system).

<br />

### Do they appear in the Purchasely Console?

They will appear in the subscription/one-time purchase listings.

Except for “family shared” subscriptions (whether unknown or known), they will appear in the various dashboards where it makes sense (typically, they will not appear in paywall dashboards if the purchases were made on an app not yet using Purchasely).

<br />

### Can I reassign an identifier to these Purchasely users?

* In the case of family sharing: when the person opens their app, the subscription will automatically be associated with them. Therefore, there is nothing you need to do.
* In the case of an error: a simple click on the “restore” link at the bottom of our paywalls will resolve the issue.
* In other cases: please contact Purchasely.

<br />

### How should I manage these subscriptions?

There is nothing special to do:

* If you are subscribed to our webhooks, we do not send them in this case.
* If you have connected integrations, likewise, nothing will be sent.

<br />

> 📘 I want to receive webhooks for unknown users
>
> No problem! Contact us and we can activate them for you
