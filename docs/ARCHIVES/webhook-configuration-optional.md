# Purpose of using a webhook

Using the webhook will allow you to receive the subscription events generated by the Purchasely Cloud Platform, on your own backend through a real-time server-to-server integration.

The webhook will be useful for 2 main purposes:

1. **managing entitlements**: if your backend is in charge of managing entitlements with your app, the webhook will allow you to know when subscription start, are renewed or terminated, in order to open or close the entitlements. It's particularly useful if the subscription proposed in the app should be accessible on multiple platforms for a same user.

   E.g.: a video app protecting videos with DRMs (Digital Right Management) will rely on the webhook to deliver the DRMs to decode the videos only to its subscribers and to ensure the multi-platform access.

   Refer to [Entitlements management](/docs/entitlements-management) to know more
2. **getting the subscription lifecycle data in your own database**: the webhook delivers realtime events that map the subscription lifecycle for every subscription. It is the best way to keep your own system up to date.

   E.g.: having your own copy of the subscription lifecycle data will allow you to make analysis of your own, or to trigger personalized workflows for your subscribers (depending on their lifecycle or revenue generated) directly from your backend

   Refer to [Subscription events](/docs/subscription-events) to know more

# Configuring the webhook

The configuration of the webhook is optional, in particular if you are running the SDK in `paywallObserver` mode. You can leave the parameter `Client webhook URL` empty. 

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/c3e93a7-image.png" />

If you configure an endpoint, every event sent on the webhook will need to be acknowledged with a `HTTP 200` response.

> 📘 Visualizing webhook events
>
> If you want to see the events generated on the webhook, you can use a free service such as [webhook.site](https://webhook.site/), which will generate a unique and temporary URL that you can use as a a Client webhook URL.
>
> *⚠️ The webhook.site URL will respond only for a few days so don't forget to renew it regularly or remove it once it's been deactivated to avoid seeing errors triggered by the SDK ⚠️*

Every event sent by Purchasely Cloud Platform on the webhook is signed using a `Client shared secret`.

Refer to [Webhook documentation](/docs/webhook) to know more.
