[Purchasely Figma plugin]() is the fastest and **easiest way to create a live native funnels in your mobile app from a Figma design**.

Purchasely's Figma plugin offers unbeatable features that you need:

* **Free form design**: With or with our auto-layout. No matter how your designers work we will turn it into a screen that matches your design.
* **Type inference**: We understand your design elements and automatically convert frames representing buttons into a real Purchasely button with all its capabilities.
* **Advanced typing**: You can override that inference, chose which is a selected state or event choose to ignore frames by setting them manually.
* **Flows**: You can export not just one screen but a 10-screen-onboarding flow in seconds

<br />

# Your first export

![]()

1. Select the frames to export
2. Open Purchasely plugin
3. Export
4. Enjoy ☕️

# Make your design smarter by typing your frames

Your designs cannot describe your app and raw exporting frames into stacks won't be enough:

* Buttons have actions and **highlighted states**
* Pickers and Quizzes have **selected states**
* Some frames are just design placeholders (status bar, dynamic island…) and **shall not be exported**

We provide an easy way for you to **type** them **directly from Figma** and make sure they will be **exported smartly** so that you don't spend time fixing that in Purchasely console.
