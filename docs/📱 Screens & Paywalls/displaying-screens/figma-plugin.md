Purchasely Figma plugin is the fastest and easiest way to move from a Figma design into a live funnel on your mobile app.

Purchasely's Figma plugin offers unbeatable features that you need:

* Free form design: With or with our auto-layout. No matter how your designers work we will turn it into a screen that matches your design.
* Type inference: We understand your design elements and automatically convert frames representing buttons into a real Purchasely button with all its capabilities.
* Manual typing: You can override that inference, chose which is a selected state or event choose to ignore frames by setting them manually.
* Flow: You can export not just one screen but a 10-screen-onboarding flow in seconds
