Purchasely offers several features to ensure your paywalls look great across all devices:

- Responsive Layouts
- Customizable sizes (fonts, spacers, components, etc.)
- Device-specific images

# Responsive layouts

Purchasely uses layouts to display screens tailored to each device type. These templates adjust to the proportions, sizes, and orientations of different devices, ensuring your paywalls look and feel optimal on smartphones, tablets, and TVs. Our paywall grids adapt dynamically to make the best use of available space.

# Customizable Sizes by Device

A 12pt font size may look perfect on a smartphone but not on a tablet or TV, where the screen is farther away. Purchasely's paywall and screen builder lets you customize sizes for each design element by device type. Simply select the device type and set your desired values. The preview will automatically switch to the selected device, allowing you to see changes instantly.
