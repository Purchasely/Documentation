Purchasely lets you preset the colours you use in your screens and paywalls. The benefits of this feature are:

1. Saves time for designers by avoiding repetitive colour selection.
2. Ensures brand colours are applied correctly across all instances.
3. Avoids error in manual entry or picking of the colour for several components at the same time
