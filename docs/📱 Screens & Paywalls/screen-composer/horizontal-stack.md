Horizontal Stacks give you the power to group components into a single block and arrange them horizontally, bringing cohesion and clarity to your layouts. You can control horizontal alignment, choose between fixed or dynamic heights, and customize the stack with background colors or images. 

For advanced designs, stacks can even be nested within each other, unlocking new levels of creative flexibility. 

This powerful feature brings greater responsiveness across devices and enables you to build visually compelling, modular screens with ease.
