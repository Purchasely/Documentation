Entitlement Events are generated by the Purchasely Platform when premium accesses are created/modified/removed (because of a purchase, renewal, refund, payment issue, ...).

There are 2 different Entitlements Events:

<Table align={["left","left","left"]}>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        Transaction Event
      </th>

      <th style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        Used to
      </th>

      <th style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        Triggered when
      </th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        `ACTIVATE`
      </td>

      <td style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        Activate the user’s entitlements
      </td>

      <td style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        <ol><li>a new subscription starts</li><li>a subscription is renewed</li><li>an In-App Purchase (consumable / non consumable) is purchased</li><li>a subscription or a non consumable is "stolen" from another user, following a restore or sign-in/sign-up</li><li>a subscription starts because of a plan change (following an up-selling, down-selling or cross-selling)</li><li>a subscription enters in grace period</li><li>a subscription is resumed after being paused (Google Play Store only)</li><li>a subscription is resumed after a billing retry or a grace period</li><li>a refund has been asked by the user via Apple Care but the developer refuses it from the App Store Connect Console (tricky one we know 😅...)</li></ol>
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        `DEACTIVATE`
      </td>

      <td style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        Deactivate the user’s entitlements
      </td>

      <td style={{ textAlign: "left" }}>
        <ol><li>a subscription is effectively terminated</li><li>a subscription enters in billing retry</li><li>a subscription or an In-App Purchase has been refunded</li><li>a subscription or a non consumable has been transferred to another user, following a restore or sign-in/sign-up</li><li>a subscription ends because of a plan change (following an up-selling, down-selling or cross-selling)</li><li>a subscription is being paused (Google Play Store only)</li></ol>
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>

<br />

They are both composed of a JSON payload which includes all the relevant data to manage entitlements ([more details on their attributes](server-events-attributes)).

For more information on how to manage entitlements with your backend, refer to [Backend entitlements](backend-entitlements).
